# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** _who was involved_

_A record that builds on one of the project template's decisions in [project-template/](project-template/README.md) adds a line here: **Refines:** project-template ADR-NNNN when it adds NCode.Identity-specific decisions to it, **Supersedes:** project-template ADR-NNNN when it replaces it (list the override in AGENTS.md), or **Conflicts with:** project-template ADR-NNNN with the issue that tracks bringing the two into line. A record carries no other relation line: when its decision changes, rewrite it in place. Status is `Proposed` or `Accepted`._

## Context

_What is the problem or the recurring question? What forces (technical, organizational) are at
play? Keep it factual, neutral, and timeless — present tense, no development-timeline narrative
(not "we shipped X then reversed it"); describe what **is** and why._

## Decision

_State the decision in one or two sentences, then elaborate. If there is a rule or heuristic,
state it plainly so it can be applied without re-reading the whole document._

## Options considered

_The alternatives and their trade-offs. Explain why the chosen option wins._

## Consequences

_What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include follow-ups and known
"you chose wrong" smells where useful._

## References

_Links to code, issues, prior art, and the records whose rules this one relies on._
